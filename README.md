# SMIF-reading-material
This repository is mainly to store and share reading material for members of SMIF. SMIF is not obligated to teach about finance, you must learn at your own pace. But, SMIF will provide all necessary resources free of charge. All of the resources came from my own personal digital library. The books are organized by difficulty, top to bottom, and build off of each other, this is so that whoever is interested in learning more about finance can follow along. I recommend starting at beginner first and moving towards advanced. Only section that does not need to be read in order is history of finance as it's chronological. I hope that whoever reads this, these books can help you on your journey into finance and understanding it at a deeper level.
-Daniel B.K.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Beginner 
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.	Learn to Earn by Peter Lynch and John Rothchild
Establishes absolute basic concepts of business, capitalism, shares, and stock market operations.
2.	How to Make Money in Stocks by William J. O'Neil
Introduces price-chart reading combined with corporate earnings growth (CAN SLIM framework).
3.	A Random Walk Down Wall Street by Burton G. Malkiel
Explains how stock and bond markets function, market efficiency, and basic asset class behavior.
4.	The Little Book of Common Sense Investing by John C. Bogle
Introduces low-cost indexing logic, compound returns, and benchmark comparison.
5.	The Bogleheads' Guide to Investing by Taylor Larimore et al.
Applies Bogle's indexing principles to setting up practical asset allocations and managing personal portfolios.
6.	Reminiscences of a Stock Operator by Edwin Lefèvre
Classic study on market psychology, tape reading, price action, and risk discipline.
7.	The Intelligent Investor by Benjamin Graham
Introduces value investing philosophy, market fluctuations (Mr. Market), and the concept of "margin of safety."
8.	How to Read a Financial Report by John A. Tracy
Teaches how income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements interconnect—essential before attempting any valuation work.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Intermediate
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.	What Has Worked in Investing by Tweedy, Browne Company
Provides historical empirical evidence on fundamental value factors (low P/E, low P/B, insider buying).
10.	Security Analysis by Benjamin Graham and David Dodd
The classical quantitative foundation for deep balance-sheet analysis, debt coverage, and intrinsic value calculation.
11.	Quality of Earnings by Thornton L. O'glove
Transitions from reading statements to detecting earnings management, non-recurring charges, and inventory build-ups.
12.	Financial Shenanigans by Howard M. Schilit, Jeremy Perler, and Yoni Engelhart
Forensic accounting manual for spotting corporate revenue manipulation and off-balance-sheet tricks.
13.	Financial Statement Analysis: A Practitioner's Guide by Martin S. Fridson and Fernando Alvarez
Advanced credit and equity analysis using corporate 10-K filings and financial ratios.
14.	Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation by Stephen Penman
Connects accounting accruals directly to formal equity valuation and residual income models.
15.	Expectations Investing by Alfred Rappaport and Michael J. Mauboussin
Reverses discounted cash flow models to estimate what future operating performance is priced into a stock.
16.	Investment Valuation: Tools and Techniques for Determining the Value of Any Asset by Aswath Damodaran
Comprehensive institutional valuation guide (DCF, WACC, relative multiples, and option pricing applications).
17.	Capital Allocation by David Giroux
Evaluates how management teams deploy free cash flow across buybacks, dividends, organic CapEx, and M&A.
18.	The Bond Book by Annette Thau
Foundational fixed-income concepts: yields, yield curves, duration, interest rate risk, and credit ratings.
19.	Mastering the Market Cycle by Howard Marks
Analyzes credit expansion, liquidity cycles, default rates, and market risk sentiment.
20.	Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets by John J. Murphy
The industry-standard reference manual for chart patterns, trendlines, volume profile, and technical indicators.
21.	Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure for Practitioners by Larry Harris
Explains how exchanges work, order matching algorithms, liquidity provision, market makers, and bid-ask spreads.
22.	Intermarket Analysis: Profiting from Global Market Relationships by John J. Murphy
Examines lead-lag relationships across equities, interest rates, foreign exchange, and physical commodities.
23.	The Alchemy of Finance by George Soros
Introduces the theory of reflexivity—how investor perceptions actively alter fundamental economic realities.
24.	The Intelligent Asset Allocator by William Bernstein
Applies Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT), calculating asset class variance, covariance, and efficient frontiers.
25.	Fortune's Formula by William Poundstone
Explores the Kelly Criterion, probability theory, optimal bet sizing, and capital growth limits.
26.	Quantitative Value by Wesley Gray and Tobias Carlisle
Systematic, algorithm-driven stock selection based on value and financial health metrics.
27.	Quantitative Momentum by Wesley Gray and Jack Vogel
Systematic factor modeling targeting price momentum persistence and trend strength.
28.	The Holy Grail of Investing by Tony Robbins & Christopher Zook
Overview of alternative asset allocation across private equity, private credit, and venture capital.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Advanced
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
29.	Competitive Strategy: Techniques for Analyzing Industries and Competitors by Michael E. Porter
Qualitative corporate analysis using Porter's Five Forces to evaluate pricing power and structural economic moats.
30.	Dear Chairman: Boardroom Battles and the Rise of Shareholder Activism by Jeff Gramm
Analyzes proxy fights, board governance, corporate agency problems, and activist investor campaigns.
31.	Creative Cash Flow Reporting by Charles W. Mulford and Eugene E. Comiskey
Advanced accounting forensics analyzing working capital manipulation and operating vs. financing cash flow distortions.
32.	Applied Mergers and Acquisitions by Robert F. Bruner
The structural, legal, tax, valuation, and accretion/dilution mechanics of major corporate M&A deals.
33.	Mastering Private Equity: Transformation via Venture Capital, Minority Investments and Buyouts by Claudia Zeisberger et al.
Institutional LBO modeling, capital structure optimization, deal sourcing, and fund-level economics.
34.	Venture Capital and the Finance of Innovation by Andrew Metrick and Ayako Yasuda
Startup valuation under extreme uncertainty, term sheets, liquidation preferences, and option pool dilution math.
35.	Distressed Debt Analysis: Strategies for Speculative Investors by Stephen G. Moyer
Chapter 11 corporate reorganizations, priority of claims, credit agreements, and workouts for defaulted debt.
36.	Fixed Income Mathematics: Analytical & Statistical Techniques by Frank J. Fabozzi
Quantitative bond analytics: modified duration, key-rate duration, convexity, and yield curve shift decomposition.
37.	Credit Risk Modeling: Theory and Applications by David Lando
Structural default modeling (Merton model), reduced-form intensity models, and pricing credit default swaps (CDS).
38.	The New Lombard Street: How the Fed Became the Dealer of Last Resort by Perry Mehrling
Institutional monetary economics: shadow banking, money market funds, repo markets, and central bank liquidity.
39.	Inefficient Markets: An Introduction to Behavioral Finance by Andrei Shleifer
Formal mathematical modeling of noise trader risk, investor sentiment anomalies, and limits to arbitrage.
40.	Expected Returns: An Investor's Guide to Harvesting Market Rewards by Antti Ilmanen
Empirical study on harvesting risk premia across asset classes, style factors (Value, Carry, Momentum), and illiquidity.
41.	Active Portfolio Management: A Quantitative Approach for Producing Superior Returns and Controlling Risk by Richard C. Grinold and Ronald N. Kahn
Mathematical framework for quantitative alpha generation, tracking error constraints, and Information Ratios.
42.	Asset Pricing by John H. Cochrane
PhD-level textbook unifying asset pricing via Stochastic Discount Factors (SDF) and generalized method of moments (GMM).
43.	Analysis of Financial Time Series by Ruey S. Tsay
Financial econometrics covering GARCH, ARIMA models, non-linear time series analysis, and high-frequency data statistical modeling.
44.	Investment Under Uncertainty by Avinash K. Dixit and Robert S. Pindyck
Real options valuation, dynamic programming, and capital budgeting decisions under stochastic market conditions.
45.	Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading by Álvaro Cartea, Sebastián Jaimungal, and José Penalva
Mathematical modeling of limit order books, optimal execution (TWAP/VWAP), inventory risk, and microsecond order routing.
